# Exeriva

**Exeriva** is an open-source platform for creating, delivering, and automatically evaluating interactive exercises for mathematics, engineering, and other STEM disciplines.

Exeriva is designed to go beyond traditional static questions by allowing instructors to create **dynamic, parameterized, and computationally evaluated problems**. Students can receive individualized problems, submit their solutions, and obtain immediate feedback.

## Vision

Exeriva aims to provide an open and extensible alternative for educators who want to build **interactive, personalized, and automatically assessed learning experiences** without depending on proprietary educational platforms.

## Key Features

* 🧮 **Interactive STEM exercises**
* 🔢 **Parameterized problems** with automatically generated variations
* ⚙️ **Automatic answer evaluation**
* 🎯 **Personalized exercises** for individual students
* 💡 **Immediate feedback**
* 📊 **Student progress and performance tracking**
* 🧩 **Extensible exercise architecture**
* 🌐 **Web-based learning environment**
* 🔓 **Open-source and self-hostable**

## Target Users

Exeriva is intended for:

* University instructors
* Schools and educators
* Students
* Researchers in engineering and STEM education
* Institutions developing digital learning environments

## Why Exeriva?

Most conventional online homework systems are either limited in their flexibility or tied to proprietary ecosystems. Exeriva seeks to provide an **open, research-friendly, and extensible platform** where educators can design their own computationally evaluated exercises and researchers can experiment with new approaches to digital STEM education.

## Research and Education

Exeriva is being developed with both **educational practice and scientific research** in mind. Its open architecture can support research into personalized learning, automated assessment, formative feedback, and computational approaches to STEM education.

## Project Status

🚧 **Active development**

Exeriva is currently under development. APIs, interfaces, and internal architectures may change as the project evolves.
